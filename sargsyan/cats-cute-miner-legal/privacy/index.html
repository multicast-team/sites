Cat Miners: Idle Tycoon Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026.

1. Operator and scope

Multicast Games Limited provides Cat Miners: Idle Tycoon for Android (com.multicastgames.catscuteminer). This policy describes this game's information practices, including the rewarded-ad version and its startup choices. The game's intended audience is aged 13 and older; the current advertising-enabled entry flow requires confirmation that the player is 16 or older, as explained below.

For privacy questions, purchase support or a request concerning a child, contact support@multicastgames.com. Our website is multicastgames.com.

2. Startup choices, age and protected play

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older. Protection for a selected younger or unknown age does not expand this intended audience.

The current Android version presents Privacy & ads on a new installation and on eligible existing installations without a recorded acceptance of the current startup notice. Its Privacy Policy and Terms of Use buttons open the game-specific documents before the player makes a choice:

  • Confirm accepts the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use, confirms that the player is 16 or older, and enables the game's rewarded-ad service. The choice is saved before gameplay and service initialization continue. Google may then present a separate privacy-choice form where required.
  • Decline closes the Android app. It does not save acceptance, enable advertising, start gameplay or initialize the game's integrated services. If no acceptance was saved, the startup choice appears again on a later launch.

Acceptance of the documents and the game's ad-service choice are separate from any choices required by Google's consent form. Confirm does not stand in for those choices and does not enable Adjust or Meta. Advertising availability and personalization depend on the applicable privacy choices and provider availability. Saved acceptance is retained separately from later advertising preferences.

Existing installations with an established protected age retain that protection. Selecting under 13, 13–15 or prefer not to say in the age settings does not enable ads, Adjust or Meta. Unknown or unreadable age records do not automatically become adult records; an explicit eligible-age confirmation is required before advertising can be enabled. Established younger age categories cannot be replaced by the ad button. Protected players' monetary purchase-revenue records are not sent to analytics providers. Existing protected play can continue without advertising; it does not expand the game's intended audience.

Players can revisit Settings → Privacy settings to control gameplay measurement and eligible additional services. The game requests an age category, not a name or full date of birth. Choosing an older category gives no extra game reward. Age, startup acceptance and privacy preferences are stored separately from progress and are not cleared merely by resetting progress. Essential purchase processing is separate from these optional services.

Earlier releases used different startup behavior: versions 0.1.3 and 0.1.4 did not require the current startup notice and kept analytics off until eligible saved choices; version 0.1.5 introduced automatic basic AppMetrica measurement. Those older screens and no-ad descriptions do not describe the current rewarded-ad version.

3. Information on the device

The game stores progress, inventory, currencies, settings, age and privacy choices, startup acceptance, local backups or restart archives, and records used to prevent duplicate purchase and ad-reward delivery. Its diagnostic logger records service states and error codes in memory and Android's system log, without receipts, passwords or account identifiers.

The payment system can store a randomly generated app-installation identifier, purchase-service session credentials and delivery records. This installation identifier is generated by the app; it is not the Android advertising ID, a phone number, IMEI or a hardware serial number. Session credentials use Android encryption and are kept outside the app's normal backup location.

Android's app-storage controls can remove local data. Resetting progress is not a data-deletion request: age protection, purchase information and local archives can remain. Removing local data does not erase Google's or our server records and can prevent this installation from recovering unfinished purchases.

4. Purchases and essential processing

Real-money purchases are optional. Google Play handles checkout and payment credentials; we do not receive card numbers or bank-account details.

For purchase processing, the game uses an app-installation identifier and a game-specific guest-account identifier. The game sends the original Google Play purchase receipt to Multicast's validation service over HTTPS. The service also processes product, order and transaction identifiers, purchase tokens, price and currency, and delivery or recovery state. Network services receive the connection's IP address when processing requests.

Access to purchase-service credentials and purchase records is restricted to authorized systems and staff. Multicast's purchase and hosting services use these records for authentication, validation, delivery, duplicate prevention, unfinished-order recovery, fraud prevention and purchase support. A child's purchase records are not sent to optional analytics providers for advertising or profiling. Google Play's billing services can connect to obtain product information and unfinished purchases, independently of the optional analytics and advertising choices.

A parent or guardian should supervise a child's purchases and configure Google Play approval settings. An in-game purchase access check does not verify parental identity or replace parental permission required by law; it is not consent to advertising or optional analytics.

5. Basic gameplay measurement and additional services

Basic AppMetrica gameplay measurement

AppMetrica basic measurement is enabled by default unless disabled in the saved settings. In the current startup flow, the game waits for the startup choice and, when advertising is requested, the MAX privacy initialization before starting the other eligible services. Decline does not start AppMetrica. Measurement covers app sessions, foreground playtime, progression, virtual-currency activity and interactions such as opening the store. Gameplay event parameters do not contain names, email addresses, birth dates, payment receipts, passwords or purchase tokens.

Before activation the game disables AppMetrica advertising-identifier tracking. Device-location collection, automatic purchase-revenue collection, crash reporting and automatic app-open/deeplink tracking are also disabled. This does not make the SDK identifier free: AppMetrica still processes app and device information, service-generated installation/device identifiers, usage events and the connection's IP address. The service can derive an approximate country or region from the IP address. These technical identifiers are distinct from a name or advertising ID.

Players can stop future AppMetrica sending in Settings → Privacy settings → Gameplay & session analytics, then Save settings. This control does not require age selection. Previously saved explicit refusals from eligible older players are retained during the update. Re-enabling measurement is optional. Disabling measurement does not itself delete data already received by the service; contact us for access or deletion assistance.

Monetary purchase-revenue measurement remains limited to eligible players who have selected 16 or older, saved their settings and have AppMetrica enabled. It uses verified transaction data; protected players' purchase-revenue records are excluded. Essential purchase validation remains separate.

Additional optional services

Only players selecting 16 or older can choose Adjust or Meta measurement. Their choices start off; declining them does not prevent ordinary play or change the basic AppMetrica preference.

  • Adjust: attribution, sessions and game activity, including progression, upgrades, virtual-currency activity and eligible purchase revenue.
  • Meta App Events: selected game events and eligible purchase-revenue measurement.

Depending on the service and choices involved, these additional services can process app and device information, IP addresses, service-generated identifiers, activity events and eligible transaction details. Adjust and Meta advertising-ID collection is disabled by the game.

Eligible older players can revisit Settings → Privacy settings → Additional services to stop future collection by Adjust or Meta, then save settings. Earlier gameplay events are not backfilled to a newly enabled provider. Withdrawing a choice does not automatically delete information already sent; contact us for deletion assistance.

Rewarded advertising and privacy choices

The current Android version uses AppLovin MAX for player-initiated rewarded ads, with AppLovin advertising demand. It does not currently display banners or interstitial ads. Advertising is restricted to eligible players who explicitly enable it. A game ad button requests an ad; it does not silently enable unrelated analytics services or remove age protection.

MAX integrates Google's User Messaging Platform for applicable privacy choices. Where a form is required and available, the native flow runs before ad loading proceeds. The game does not preselect or override the player's choices. Acknowledging these documents is not blanket consent to personalized advertising. Depending on consent, region and provider support, advertisements may be limited or unavailable; Confirm does not guarantee that an ad will be available.

Advertising services can process app and device information, IP address and approximate region, technical identifiers including an Android advertising ID where available and permitted, ad requests, impressions, interactions, completion and diagnostic information. These are used for delivering and measuring advertising, permitted personalization, security and fraud prevention. MAX supplies ad-performance and revenue information; any forwarding to an additional analytics provider follows that provider's separate eligibility and saved choice. The game's receipt and guest-account credentials are not ad-reward parameters. The AppMetrica advertising-ID and location restrictions described above do not mean that an enabled advertising SDK processes no identifiers.

Eligible players can turn Rewarded ads off under Settings → Privacy settings → Additional services and save. The Manage ad privacy control opens the provider's privacy-choice flow where supported after initialization. These controls affect future activity; already received information is subject to the relevant provider's policies and applicable rights. Android's advertising-ID controls provide additional device-level choices. Disabling ads later does not undo the recorded acceptance of the documents or require a new ad watch to continue ordinary play.

Google describes its platform and consent-service practices in the Google Privacy Policy. Provider notices are available from AppLovin, Adjust, Meta and Yandex / AppMetrica. Ad providers can share information with their service and advertising partners as described in their notices and subject to applicable choices.

6. Retention

Local data remains until removed through the relevant app or Android controls; some reset archives and essential purchase or privacy records are retained as described above.

Server purchase-record retention is tied to order authentication and delivery, recovery or support, fraud prevention, and applicable legal, tax or accounting obligations. When records are no longer required for these purposes, they are deleted or anonymized, unless a longer retention period is required by law. An unresolved purchase or a legal retention duty can prevent immediate deletion of the affected transaction records.

7. Requests from players, parents and guardians

Our studio handles privacy requests through the support contact below, including requests concerning purchase records. You can also use our data request page.

To request access, correction or deletion of information concerning you or a child in your care, email support@multicastgames.com, identifying the game and describing the request. An order reference may help locate purchase records. Do not send passwords, card details or a child's full date of birth.

We may need proportionate information to establish that a request concerns the right records and the person entitled to make it. Legal retention duties or an unresolved purchase issue may limit immediate deletion. You can also ask us to explain which records remain and why. Protected users' ordinary play does not depend on agreeing to optional collection.

8. International processing and rights

Services can process information outside your country. Applicable privacy rights may include access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, and withdrawal of consent. Contact us to exercise a right or raise a concern about a child's information. Where applicable, you may also contact the relevant data-protection authority.

9. Updates

Updates will identify the effective version of this game-specific policy. Changes requiring a new permission or consent must be handled before the affected optional processing begins.
