Returning from an ad is not a new AFK period and does not itself create an offline-income or Double Income offer. Genuine time away can create the normal offline-income offer under the game's rules. The offered ad reward and ordinary offline progress are separate.

After entering the game, eligible players can disable Rewarded ads in Settings → Privacy settings → Additional services and save settings, and can revisit supported provider choices with Manage ad privacy.
