01 / The two of us

Two personalities.
One shared cause.

Both are originally from Murmansk. Each found their own way to making games — through engineering, user support, testing and project management.

Company
and user acquisition

Vyacheslav Krivsunov

Co-founder of Multicast Games. In the Persona.ly case study of 25 May 2023 he is named CEO and Head of UA, leading both the company and player acquisition. [6 · 2023]

His professional path goes beyond game development: first system administration and engineering work in the Far North, then project management and producing. His VK profile also lists ASO and product development among his current areas. [13]

  1. 2008–2010
    System administrator and programmer in Kovdor, then systems engineer at Murmanset.
  2. 2015–2019
    Project manager at Kefir, later Product Owner and producer.
  3. Since 2019
    Producing at GbG; Multicast Games listed on his profile since 2021.
Professional profile ↗ 3D portfolio ↗

Production
and game development

Inna Krivsunova

Co-founder of Multicast Games and producer of Zombie City Master. In the AppTractor interview of 22 May 2024 she is introduced as COO. [1 · 2024]

She started in technical support, then moved into game testing. She worked at Sperasoft on the PlayStation Store, at Game Insight and at Thematica, and later moved into project management and producing. [3 · 2022]

  1. 2015–2016
    Technical support at SKB Kontur, then Sperasoft and a move into QA.
  2. 2017–2018
    Game Insight and Social Quantum — hands-on experience with game projects.
  3. 2019–2021
    Project management at Green Button Games; Multicast Games since 2021. [14]

Guest speaker at a 2023 game-dev intensive on product development, marketing and monetization. [11 · 2023]

04 / Places and impressions

From Murmansk
to our beloved Dubai.

We are both from Murmansk. The North is part of our story, and for more than a year now Dubai has been our home.

Our home in Dubai Marina

We have been living in Dubai for over a year, at Dorra Bay in Dubai Marina. Pupa, Lupa and Nix live here with us.

Now we have decided to get a dog and move somewhere more spacious and quiet. Ahead of us: a new home and one more family member. [25]

  • Murmansk
  • Yerevan
  • Dubai

Why we love Dubai

We really love Dubai and its spirit of progress and growth. Here new technologies become part of everyday life — and that feeling of moving forward is close to our hearts.

We enjoy living in a city that keeps trying new things, growing and changing. And alongside that rhythm, we want to create a spacious, calm home of our own. [25]

♡

Yerevan × Murmansk

Helping those still waiting for a home.

We sponsor cat and dog shelters in Yerevan and Murmansk. For us, caring for animals goes beyond our own family. [25]

05 / The studio story

Every game
has a backstory.

First projects, mobile experiments, a studio of our own and games played by millions.

↳
  1. 2014

    First joint projects

    On 7 December a Red Rage thread appeared on GameDev.ru. In an interview, Inna also recalls Operation JuggerFall as one of their first joint projects. [7 · 2014] [3 · 2022]

  2. ≈2017

    Mobile games in their spare time

    According to Inna, the mobile team started with five people. Their first hit was Fish Idle. [1 · 2024]

  3. 2021

    The company is founded

    The Multicast Games profile lists 2021. That is the company's date, not the start of their work together. [8]

  4. 2022

    A new chapter for Zombie City Master

    In May the game was sold to Tilting Point. Inna later talked about the project's history and the deal in an interview. [3 · 2022] [9 · 2022]

  5. 2023

    Alien Invasion in industry case studies

    Persona.ly and AppMetrica publish results of their work on the game: monetization, player acquisition and analytics become a story of their own within the studio's history. [6 · 2023] [4 · 2023]

  6. 2024

    From an experiment to a big team

    In an interview Inna explains that Alien Invasion grew out of an experiment with Fish Idle's visuals and was sold to CrazyLabs. At the time of the interview the studio had 25 employees. [1 · 2024]

  7. 2026

    The story continues

    The official website reports 20M+ portfolio downloads. The portfolio includes Alien Invasion, Fish Idle, Butcher Hero and other games. [2 · 2026]
